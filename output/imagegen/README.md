# Wedding landscape artwork

Created using the built-in image_gen tool for Forever Begins. The screenshot supplied by Udeshya was used as conceptual inspiration; its photographs and UI were not copied.

## Deliverables

- public/artwork/waterfalls-meet-the-sea.jpg — home panorama, inner-page hero background, invitation artwork.
- public/artwork/ranchi-waterfall.jpg — Udeshya's hometown card and story illustration.
- public/artwork/vizag-coast.jpg — Bhanu's hometown card and story illustration.
- Original PNGs are preserved in this directory. JPEGs keep their original dimensions and use quality 85 compression for the website.

These are AI-generated, painterly interpretations. They are not photographs of actual landmarks, wedding venues, the couple, or college memories. The panorama imaginatively connects two landscapes; it is not a map. Real photographs belong in the gallery.

## Concept

Where waterfalls meet the sea. Ranchi and Visakhapatnam are the roots; the college meeting in Odisha is the shared beginning. No new relationship milestones or family traditions have been invented.

Official city-name references:
- Ranchi, City of Water Falls: https://ranchi.nic.in/about-district/
- Visakhapatnam, City of Destiny: https://visakhapatnam.ap.gov.in/
